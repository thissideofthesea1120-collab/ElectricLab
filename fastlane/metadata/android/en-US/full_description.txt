ElectricLab is an offline virtual electricity laboratory for middle and high school physics.

Drag power sources, switches, lamps, resistors, rheostats, ammeters, voltmeters, electromagnets and other apparatus onto the bench. Connect wires yourself, close the switch and observe current, voltage, brightness and magnetic effects.

Features:
• Free wiring with terminals, bends, rewiring and crossing warnings
• Circuit simulation for series, parallel, rheostats, motors, buzzers and LEDs
• Meter reading practice with ranges, polarity, reversal and overload feedback
• Experiment templates for common classroom demonstrations
• 15 graded challenge tasks with data tables and conclusions
• Capacitors and inductors with charge/discharge observation
• Selectable meter internal resistance (analog/digital/high precision)
• Adjustable source internal resistance and AC/DC student supply
• Magnets, solenoids, electromagnets and compass
• Snapshots and measurement records

The app is fully offline, has no ads and requests no network permission.
